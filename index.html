Study Timetable

Study factor = real study minutes per minute of video. Tap a class to mark it done, and the moon moves it to that evening (20:00 to midnight). The plan updates itself every day: missed classes are rescheduled automatically. In the week view, drag the top grip of a class to move it (this changes its breaks) and the bottom grip to make it longer or shorter. Only that class changes, and double-clicking a grip resets the day. Breaks between evening classes are 10 minutes.
